Symptom Checker

What is your MacBook doing?

These are the most common symptoms I see. If yours matches, text me and I will tell you if it is a quick fix or something bigger.

Screen is cracked or black
If the glass is shattered but you still see the image, it may just need a display replacement. If it is completely black, the issue could be the display cable or logic board.
Won't hold a charge
Batteries degrade after 500–1000 cycles. If your MacBook dies at 30% or swells against the trackpad, it is time for a replacement. Swelling can damage the trackpad and chassis.
Keyboard keys stuck or repeating
Butterfly keyboards are notorious for stuck and double-typing keys. Dust, crumbs, or liquid can cause this. Some models qualify for Apple's program — I can check and advise.
Won't turn on at all
No power can mean a dead battery, failed logic board, damaged charging port, or liquid corrosion. I run a full diagnostic to pinpoint the exact component before quoting.
Liquid spilled on it
Turn it off immediately and do not charge it. Bring it in as fast as possible — corrosion starts within hours. The sooner I clean it, the higher the chance of full recovery.
Loud fans / overheating
Thermal issues often mean dust-clogged vents, dried thermal paste, or a runaway process. I deep-clean the cooling system and reapply thermal paste when needed.
