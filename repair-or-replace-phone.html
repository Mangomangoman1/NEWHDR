Repair math, not guesswork

Repair or Replace Your Phone?

If your phone still does what you need and the repair is clearly cheaper than replacement, repair usually wins. If the repair cost approaches the used replacement value, multiple major parts are failing, or the phone is out of support, replacement is usually the cleaner move.

Repair usually wins when

  • The issue is isolated. One cracked screen, tired battery, bad charging port, or camera failure is often worth quoting.
  • Your data matters. Repair can be the safest path when backup is uncertain.
  • You like the phone. If it still feels fast and gets updates, fixing it can beat restarting with another device.

Replace usually wins when

  • Multiple major parts failed. Screen plus frame plus battery plus board damage can cross the line.
  • Liquid damage is severe. Corrosion can make a phone unreliable even after one symptom is fixed.
  • Support is ending. If updates and app support are fading, money may be better spent on a newer phone.

Ask HDR when it’s close

Text the model and symptoms before buying another phone. Samuel can often tell you whether the likely repair path is sensible before you spend money.

The Wood River Valley factor

For Hailey, Ketchum, Sun Valley, and Bellevue customers, replacement often means driving, shipping, data transfer, setup time, and another case/screen protector. A repair quote should be compared against the total hassle, not only the sticker price of another phone.

Questions this page answers

Fast answers for AI and humans

These are written plainly so customers, search engines, and answer engines can extract the right repair guidance without guessing.

Is it worth repairing a cracked phone screen?

Usually yes if the phone is otherwise working, the frame is not badly bent, and the screen repair is far below the cost of replacing the phone. Text HDR the exact model and a photo so the quote is based on the right part.

When should I replace instead of repair?

Replace when the repair is close to the cost of a similar used phone, the device has multiple major failures, update support is ending, or liquid/board damage makes reliability uncertain.

Can HDR help me decide before I bring it in?

Yes. Text the model, symptoms, photos, and whether data matters. HDR can usually give a practical repair-versus-replace direction before you schedule drop-off or mail-in.
