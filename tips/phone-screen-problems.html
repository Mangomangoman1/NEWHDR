Phone screen problems:
find yours by what you see

Samuel, Owner & Technician · November 9, 2026

Most screen faults look alike at first: something is dark, cracked, or behaving strangely. Pick the line that matches what you can see. Each one goes to a single guide with safe tests in the right order.

Three things change the first step.

The screen or back is liftingIt may be a swollen battery. Stop charging it.The phone got wetUnplug it and power it off before any screen test.The photos are not backed upCheck what is already saved before trying fixes.

03 / What you see

The picture or touch is wrong

5 guides

  1. 07A green, white or pink line on an iPhoneTake a screenshot and view it on another device to tell a display fault from software.
  2. 08Samsung green line, flicker, dead touch or burn-inWhat each AMOLED symptom points to, what to try first, and when it is the screen.
  3. 09A white or bright spot that stays in one placeTell an LCD backlight pressure mark from a stuck pixel with a screenshot and a color test.
  4. 10The iPhone taps, types or opens apps by itselfStop the accidental input first, then test the case, protector, charger and moisture.
  5. 11Stop using it
    The screen is lifting away from the frameA lifting screen or separating back can mean a swollen lithium battery.
