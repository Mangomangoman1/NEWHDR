Phone screen problems:
find yours by what you see

Samuel, Owner & Technician · November 9, 2026

Most screen faults look alike at first: something is dark, cracked, or behaving strangely. Pick the line that matches what you can see. Each one goes to a single guide with safe tests in the right order.

01 / What you see

The screen is dark

5 guides

  1. 01Black screen, but the phone still rings or vibratesCommon after a drop. The guide covers the safe first checks, the one restart worth trying, and how to tell a fixable screen from a board fault.
  2. 02Black screen after the phone got wetUnplug it, skip the charger, and test for signs of life. On iPhones the display is usually what died.
  3. 03No ring, no buzz, nothing at all after a dropDo one forced restart and an hour on a known-good charger, then stop. It may be the port, the battery or the board.
  4. 04iPhone stuck on the Apple logo, or restarting in a loopIdentify the pattern, use the correct force restart, and try recovery mode without erasing first.
  5. 05iPhone restarts about every three minutesIt may be logging a repeating panic. Protect the data and avoid erasing before the hardware is diagnosed.
02 / What you see

The glass is cracked

6 guides

  1. 06Cracked, but touch still worksTell a hairline glass crack from display damage, when a protector helps, and when it is time to repair.
  2. 07Not sure if it is the screen or only the protectorFind the protector’s edge and check the picture without pressing on the crack.
  3. 08It is an iPad with a cracked screenApple’s safety advice, two tests for display damage, and which iPad models can have the glass replaced on its own.
  4. 09The back of the iPhone is crackedWhen a case is a fair stopgap, and the signs to stop: heat, a bent frame, or camera damage.
  5. 10The back of a Samsung or Pixel is crackedWhat the back glass protects, five things to check before a case goes on, and why a lifting back is a battery warning.
  6. 11The camera lens on the back is crackedTake one photo with each lens to tell cracked cover glass from a damaged camera, and keep dust out until it’s fixed.
03 / What you see

The picture or touch is wrong

7 guides

  1. 12A green, white or pink line on an iPhoneTake a screenshot and view it on another device to tell a display fault from software.
  2. 13Samsung green line, flicker, dead touch or burn-inWhat each AMOLED symptom points to, what to try first, and when it is the screen.
  3. 14A white or bright spot that stays in one placeTell an LCD backlight pressure mark from a stuck pixel with a screenshot and a color test.
  4. 15A black spot, ink blot or half the screen blackPanel damage that can spread: back up first, confirm it with a screenshot viewed on another device, and don’t press on it.
  5. 16The iPhone taps, types or opens apps by itselfStop the accidental input first, then test the case, protector, charger and moisture.
  6. 17Picture is fine, but touch is dead, patchy or laggy after a dropClear the protector and case, restart once, map the dead zone, then get the data off before a screen repair.
  7. 18Stop using it
    The screen is lifting away from the frameA lifting screen or separating back can mean a swollen lithium battery.

After a drop or a repair
