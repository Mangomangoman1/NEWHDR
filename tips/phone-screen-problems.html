Phone screen problems:
find yours by what you see

Samuel, Owner & Technician · November 9, 2026

Most screen faults look alike at first: something is dark, cracked, or behaving strangely. Pick the line that matches what you can see. Each one goes to a single guide with safe tests in the right order.

The picture or touch is wrong

4 guides

  1. 07A green, white or pink line on an iPhoneTake a screenshot and view it on another device to tell a display fault from software.

  4. 10Stop using it
    The screen is lifting away from the frameA lifting screen or separating back can mean a swollen lithium battery.
