Green, white, or vertical line on an iPhone screen

By Samuel — owner & device repair technician · Published September 4, 2026

The color gets attention; the pattern gives the diagnosis. One off-device screenshot can quickly separate the rendered image from the physical screen.

Direct answer: Back up the iPhone and do not press or flex the display. Restart once, then take a screenshot and view it on another phone or computer. If the line is not present in that copy—and it appears across apps or during startup—the physical display, connector, or display path is the likely problem and usually needs screen service.

OLED and LCD displays address the image in rows and columns. When a column, driver, flex connection, or signal path fails, the visible result can be a razor-thin green line, a bright white band, several colored stripes, or a flickering region. The cover glass may look perfect because the failed electronics sit beneath it.

Protect access before testing the screen

  1. Make a current backup while touch and image still work. Display faults can expand without warning.
  2. Do not push the line, twist the phone, or massage the panel. A temporary flicker is not a repair.
  3. Remove a tight case only if the display is flat. If a screen edge is lifting, stop and treat possible battery swelling as a safety issue.
  4. Keep it dry. If the line followed liquid exposure, power down when practical and avoid charging.
  5. Restart once. For a frozen current-generation iPhone, press Volume Up, press Volume Down, then hold the Side button until the Apple logo appears.
Stop normal use if the screen is lifting, hot, leaking black areas, or flashing intensely. Lifting may indicate a swollen battery; OLED flicker can also be uncomfortable or unsafe for light-sensitive users.

The off-device screenshot test

Take a screenshot while the line is visible. Send or synchronize it to another device, then view the image there. Do not judge the screenshot only on the affected iPhone because the same faulty panel is displaying it.

A
The line is absent on the other device

The iPhone generated a normal image, but its physical display did not reproduce it correctly. Focus on the display panel, connector, flex, frame pressure, liquid, impact, or prior installation.

B
The line appears inside the screenshot

The line was part of the generated content. Check whether it exists only in one app, image, website, video, overlay, or accessibility feature. Software or content becomes more plausible.

C
You cannot capture or transfer a screenshot

Photograph the affected screen with another device and record whether the line is present during the Apple logo, lock screen, Settings, and several apps.

This test is powerful but not absolute. A display-path or board-level signal fault after the screenshot is generated can still require deeper diagnosis; the result primarily tells you whether the defect is baked into the captured image.

What the line’s behavior suggests

Fixed in the same location everywhereA display column, panel driver, flex, connector, or signal path is likely. The line color alone does not identify which component.
Appears during the Apple logoBecause it exists before ordinary apps load, an individual app is unlikely to be responsible.
Changes when the phone is moved or warmedAn intermittent connection or failing display can react to flex and temperature. Do not repeat the stress to prove it.
Several lines plus black or ink-like spreadingThe image layer has physical damage even if the outer glass is intact. Back up and avoid pressure; the usable area can shrink.
Line plus dead or false touchMultiple layers of the display assembly may be damaged. Protect the passcode from ghost touch and plan complete display service.
Only at one brightness or after an updateRestart and update after backup, but keep hardware in the diagnosis. Display control changes can expose marginal panels without software physically creating impact damage.

Why a line can appear without cracked glass

Impact

A drop can damage the OLED/LCD layer, driver bond, flex, or connector while leaving the cover glass unbroken. Corner and frame impacts matter.

Pressure or bending

Sitting on the phone, a tight mount, packed bag, or bent frame can stress the panel and its edge connections.

Liquid or corrosion

Moisture can affect display connectors and board-level image paths. A screen replacement alone may not resolve continuing corrosion.

Part or installation trouble

A replacement display can fail, arrive defective, be stressed by a bent frame, have a connector issue, or require calibration and post-repair checks.

Component failure

Displays can fail without a dramatic accident. Age, manufacturing variation, heat cycles, and internal electrical faults can surface as a line.

Why resets are often a distraction

A normal restart and current compatible iOS update are reasonable, low-risk software checks after backup. A factory reset is different: it erases the device and does not reconnect a flex, repair a pixel column, reverse corrosion, straighten a frame, or replace a failed panel.

If the line is missing from the off-device screenshot, exists during startup, and followed impact or pressure, do not erase merely to satisfy a generic troubleshooting script. Preserve the data and have the display path tested.

What proper screen diagnosis includes

  • Exact iPhone model, display type, and repair history
  • Line position, color, width, flicker, and screenshot result
  • Full touch-grid test and check for uncontrolled touches
  • Brightness, color, True Tone, ambient-light, and proximity behavior
  • Glass, frame, pressure, drop, and liquid inspection
  • Battery swelling or separation beneath the display
  • Parts and Service History and required calibration after repair

When the panel electronics are responsible, replacing the complete display assembly is the normal repair. “Glass-only” work cannot fix a failed OLED/LCD column or touch layer. If a known-good display shows the same fault, connector and board-level diagnosis must continue rather than installing more screens.

Ask for a full post-repair test. A successful screen job verifies image, touch across the entire surface, cameras, Face ID, proximity behavior, brightness, speakers, and charging—not only whether the colored line disappeared.

FAQ

Is a green line caused by an iOS update?

The timing may coincide with an update, and software checks are worthwhile. A fixed line absent from an off-device screenshot usually indicates the physical display path rather than pixels drawn by iOS.

Can I keep using the phone?

Temporarily, if it is flat, cool, dry, touch is controlled, and the image remains usable. Back up immediately because display faults can spread. Stop if there is lifting, heat, liquid, or false touch.

Will a screen protector fix or hide the line?

No. A protector only covers the outer glass. It cannot repair the image-producing panel or its electrical connection.

Can a connector reseat fix it?

Sometimes a connection is involved, particularly after impact or repair, but opening the iPhone requires safe battery disconnection, inspection, new sealing materials, and testing. Persistent panel lines often require the display itself.
