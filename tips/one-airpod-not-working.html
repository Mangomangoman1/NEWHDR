One AirPod
not working?

Hailey Device Repair · October 6, 2026

Start with the symptom, then change one thing.

A silent earbud, a quiet earbud, and an earbud that never charges need different checks. Choose your symptom below before resetting anything.

For AirPods and AirPods Pro with an iPhone or iPad. Excludes AirPods Max. Menu labels can vary with software version.

Stop before testing damaged hardware.Do not wear or charge an earbud or case that is unusually hot, swollen, cracked open, or still wet. Arrange assessment instead of opening it or probing the contacts.
01 / Power

Check each earbud’s charge

If the case is depleted or will not charge, put both earbuds inside, close it, and connect its correct Lightning or USB-C cable to a working adapter and outlet for at least 15 minutes. A cable test also removes wireless-pad positioning from the comparison. See Apple’s charging checks.

With a charged case, seat both earbuds normally for 30 seconds. Open it near your iPhone or iPad and look for each earbud’s charging status, following Apple’s one-earbud check. Write down left, right, and case readings; one percentage is not enough evidence that both earbuds are charging.

One side stays missing or empty
Remove and gently reseat that earbud once. If its reading still does not appear or rise after the charging check, record that result. Sound settings cannot establish whether this is a contact, battery, or case fault.
Both sides show charge
Move to the listening test. A charge reading is useful evidence, but does not prove that the speaker works or the battery holds charge normally.

For loose debris at the case’s external charging port, Apple specifies a clean, dry, soft brush. Do not insert tools into charging ports, scrape metal contacts, or add liquid to the earbud wells. If debris is stuck, stop and get help.

02 / Sound

Test the silent side by itself

  1. Put the suspect AirPod in its matching ear. Leave the other in the case and shut the lid.
  2. Confirm the AirPods are connected to your iPhone or iPad, then play familiar audio at a comfortable level.
  3. If it works alone, return both to the case for 30 seconds, then test the pair again.

This sequence comes from Apple’s left-or-right AirPod guidance. If the fault only occurs in one app, compare another app before changing hardware. Keep the volume comfortable rather than raising it to compensate.

What the result meansSound from the suspect side demonstrates that it can play during this test. It does not rule out an intermittent fault. If it remains silent with a charge reading, continue to the model-specific reset below.

If callers cannot hear you but music plays in both ears, that is a different symptom. Use the iPhone microphone guide to compare calls with and without the accessory.

03 / Quiet or muffled

Check balance before cleaning

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Accessibility → Audio & Visual → Balance. For this comparison, the slider should be centered. If you intentionally use a different balance, note it first so you can restore your preference.

Inspect the speaker mesh. On AirPods Pro, remove the detachable ear tip to see it. Do not push a pin through the mesh or use abrasive tools. For Pro ear tips, Apple permits rinsing the removed tips with water, without soap; dry them completely before refitting.

Cleaning is model-specific. Open Apple’s illustrated instructions for AirPods or AirPods Pro and match your model to the marked areas before starting. Some models have a prescribed liquid-assisted mesh procedure; that is not permission to wet every opening. Those procedures require complete drying for at least two hours before use or returning earbuds to the case.

If you cannot identify the model or safely remove debris, skip cleaning and seek service. Repeating an aggressive cleaning attempt can make a recoverable sound problem worse.

04 / Reconnect

Reset using the right case controls

First identify your AirPods model: Settings → Bluetooth → the information button beside the AirPods. If unavailable, compare the A-number printed underneath an earbud with Apple’s list. A similar-looking case does not establish compatibility.

Before resettingThis resets AirPods settings and requires reconnection; note your custom controls first. It does not call for erasing your iPhone, photos, or messages. Do not choose Erase All Content and Settings or reset the phone’s network settings for this guide.

Follow Apple’s reset sequence: put both earbuds in the case, close it for 30 seconds, then in Bluetooth settings choose the AirPods information button and Forget This Device if listed. Reopen the lid and use the matching control below.

Back setup button

AirPods 1, 2, 3
AirPods Pro 1, 2

Hold the rear setup button for about 15 seconds. Look for an amber flash followed by white.

Front tap control

AirPods 4, 5
AirPods Pro 3

Double-tap the front while the light is on; double-tap again when it flashes white; double-tap a third time when flashing speeds up. Look for amber, then white.

With the lid open, bring the case near your device and follow the connection prompts. Retest the same audio. If the expected light sequence never appears, use Apple’s linked “doesn’t flash white” instructions; do not keep inventing longer button holds.

05 / A useful stopping point

Still failing? Take the observations with you.

If one side stays silent after charging, the isolated listening test, and the correct reset—or remains quiet after balance and appropriate cleaning—contact Apple Support for service options. These checks narrow the symptom; they cannot confirm an internal battery or speaker diagnosis.

Have the model, affected side, battery readings, case charging method, and reset-light result ready. Mention drops, liquid exposure, and whether the problem happens in every app. Include both earbuds and the case when asked to bring the set for assessment.

Recently replaced one side? Follow Apple’s replacement setup procedure before treating it as a new failure. Match the earbud and case models before buying another part; even AirPods 4 cases differ between ANC and non-ANC versions.
