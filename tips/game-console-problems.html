PS5, Xbox and Switch problems:
find yours by symptom

By Samuel, owner & technician · November 9, 2026

Console faults are easy to make worse: a forced HDMI plug or a hasty reset costs more than the original problem. Find the symptom, run the safe checks, and protect your saves first.
