Laptop problems:
find yours by symptom

Samuel, Owner & Technician · November 9, 2026

Laptop faults overlap: a black screen can be the display, the cable, or a startup problem. These guides separate them with tests you can do at a desk, and say plainly where to stop.

01 / What you see

Screen and display

4 guides

  1. 01Screen black, but the fans are runningTry brightness, Win+Ctrl+Shift+B and an external monitor to tell a bad screen from a startup problem.
  2. 02Bright bars along the bottom of a MacBook Pro screenOn 2016–2017 models this points to a worn backlight cable. Confirm it with a screenshot and lid test.
  3. 03External monitor stays black on a MacBookCheck input, the video connection, Detect Displays and your model’s limits.
  4. 04Hinge popping, stiff, or pulling the case apartThe mounts inside the housing often broke, not the hinge. Tightening will not fix it.

Keyboard, touchpad, camera and sound

6 guides

  1. 10Some keys do not workCompare apps, keyboard settings and an external keyboard before replacing keys.
  2. 11Touchpad not workingCheck Windows 11 settings, gestures and official drivers. Stop if the pad is raised or swollen.
  3. 12Webcam black or not detectedCheck the shutter, Windows 11 permissions, apps and model-specific drivers.
  4. 13MacBook has no soundCompare apps, check the built-in output, and disconnect audio accessories.
  5. 14Mic works in Windows, but not on callsMatch the app input, Windows privacy and site permissions before changing drivers.
  6. 15Bluetooth headphones connected, but silentCheck output, Volume mixer, multipoint and pairing on Windows 11.
