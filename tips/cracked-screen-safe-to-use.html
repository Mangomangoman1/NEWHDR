Cracked screen: is it safe to keep using?

By Samuel — owner & technician, Hailey Device Repair · Updated October 9, 2026

Working touch and a clear image do not make broken glass safe. Here's how to recognize warning signs, understand a temporary covering, and arrange the right repair.

Direct answer: A cracked screen can injure you even if touch and the image still work. Apple advises against using an iPhone with cracked glass; arrange an assessment and repair. A properly fitted protector may reduce finger contact with a small, flat, stable crack as a temporary barrier, but it cannot guarantee safety or stop damage spreading.

Phone glass, touch sensing, and the image-producing LCD or OLED are parts of a display assembly. Glass can break while touch and the image keep working; the appearance of a crack alone cannot tell you whether other parts were damaged.

Apple's safety guidance warns that cracked glass may cause injury. Follow your phone manufacturer's precautions even when the crack looks minor.

Hairline crack or display damage — what cracked?

  • Cracked glass with normal touch and image. The display may still function, but the glass is damaged. Normal operation is not proof of safety or that only the glass was affected.
  • Image or touch problems. Dark patches, lines, discoloration, flicker, or unresponsive areas after an impact can indicate display or touch damage. Inspection is needed to identify the affected parts.
  • Glass and display damage together. Visible cracks plus image or touch faults need assessment for screen replacement and any other damage from the impact.
At HDR, screen repairs use complete bonded display assemblies, not glass-only refurbishment. Glass damage with working touch and a working OLED or LCD still calls for an assembly replacement here.

Warning signs that need prompt assessment

  • Dark patches, color shifts, or new lines — possible display damage
  • Dead touch areas, flicker, or ghost input — the phone may become unreliable or difficult to unlock
  • New cracks or loose fragments — worsening glass damage and a possible cut risk
  • A lifting screen, bulging enclosure, or unusual heat — possible battery damage as well as screen damage
A lifting or swollen screen is not a protector job. Stop using and charging the phone, power it off if you can do so safely, and seek professional help. Do not press the screen back down or cover it to hold it in place. iFixit explains how battery swelling can push a display out of alignment.

Cut risk and a temporary covering

Cracked phone glass can leave sharp edges or loose fragments. Inspect visually; do not swipe a finger across the crack to test whether it is sharp.

A properly fitted screen protector may reduce direct finger contact with a small, flat, stable crack while you arrange repair. It is a temporary barrier, not a repair or a guarantee against cuts or spreading. Applying or removing a covering can put stress on damaged glass and worsen it; do not force a protector to fit or press bubbles out over the crack.

Do not apply a protector or tape to raised, loose, or shedding glass, or to a lifting or swollen screen. Avoid handling the damaged surface and have a technician assess it instead.

iFixit's taping guide describes containing glass during repair. It does not establish that daily use under tape or a protector is safe, and a covering does not override the manufacturer's advice to stop using cracked glass.

Cracked screen and water resistance

Screen or frame damage may compromise water resistance. A visible crack does not tell you which seals remain intact, so do not rely on the phone's original IP rating. Apple notes that water resistance is not permanent and advises avoiding drops and impacts.

Keep a damaged phone away from rain, snow, pools, and river water in the Wood River Valley. A protector or tape does not restore the seals or make the phone waterproof.

If the phone gets wet, stop charging it and follow the water-damage guide. Moisture exposure with screen damage needs extra care.

When to arrange repair

Arrange an assessment even for a small crack. A clear image and working touch do not remove the broken-glass risk. Prioritize loose or sharp glass, spreading damage, and any change in touch or the image.

Why avoid delay: damage may worsen unpredictably, or the phone may lose touch or image function before you can access important data. A temporary covering does not prevent those faults.

What affects the price at HDR: the phone model, replacement assembly, and any separate damage found during inspection. Screen repair starts at $145 on Androids and $100 on iPhones. Because HDR replaces the bonded assembly in either case, a working OLED later failing does not by itself turn the same screen job into a more expensive type of replacement. Separate battery, frame, or other damage can affect the quote.

FAQ

Is it safe to keep using a phone with a cracked screen?

Working touch and a clear image do not make cracked glass safe. Apple advises against using an iPhone with cracked glass because it may cause injury. Arrange assessment and repair, and follow your phone manufacturer's precautions.

Will a cracked screen get worse on its own?

It may worsen, but the timing and extent are unpredictable. A small crack does not prove the display is failing, and continued use is not a reliable test of whether the damage will spread.

Will a screen protector stop the crack from spreading?

No protector can guarantee that. A properly fitted protector may reduce finger contact with a small, flat, stable crack as a temporary barrier. Application or removal can worsen damage. Do not apply one to raised, loose, or shedding glass, or a lifting or swollen screen; get professional assessment.

How much does a cracked screen repair cost?

HDR screen repairs start at $145 for Android models and $100 for iPhones; the exact model and replacement assembly determine the quote. HDR uses complete bonded display assemblies, not glass-only refurbishment, whether the original display still works or has failed. OLED failure alone does not change the replacement type; separate damage can affect the quote. Text the model and a photo for a specific quote.

Does cracking the screen void the warranty?

Standard manufacturer warranties generally exclude accidental screen damage; that does not automatically void all coverage. AppleCare+ may cover accidental screen damage subject to the plan's terms and a service fee. Check your coverage and repair options before booking.
