Twin Falls has repair shops, and sometimes one of them is your best move. Here’s the straight version.

Driving up makes sense when

  • you want it fixed same-day while you wait

  • you’re headed up-valley anyway — Sun Valley trip, camping, family

A Twin Falls shop makes sense when

  • you need it fixed in the next hour, in town
  • you already have a local tech you trust

Twin Falls FAQ

Questions Magic Valley customers ask first

How far is Hailey from Twin Falls?

About 80 miles — roughly 1 hour 20 minutes north on US-93 and ID-75. Close enough that many Magic Valley customers drive it once; easy enough to skip the drive entirely and mail the device.

Do I have to make two trips if I drive up?

No. Most driving customers drop off once and the repaired device ships back with free return shipping. If the part is staged ahead of time, many screen and battery jobs are finished the same day while you wait or run errands in Hailey.

Can a repair really be same-day if I drive up?

Often, yes — but text first. If the exact part can be staged on the bench before you leave Twin Falls, most screens and batteries take under an hour once the device arrives.

How long does mail-in take from Twin Falls?

USPS Priority Mail from Twin Falls usually reaches Hailey in 1–2 days. Most straightforward repairs are back in your hands in 3–5 total days, with tracking texted in both directions.

Is there a CSI student discount?

Yes. College of Southern Idaho students get 15% off any repair with a student ID. Active-duty military and veterans get the same. Details here.

What about Jerome, Burley, Kimberly, Filer, or Rupert?

The whole Magic Valley uses the same process: text the model and the problem, get a quote before any work, then drive up or mail in — whichever fits your week.

What should I text to get a quote?

The exact model, what broke, whether it still powers on, and a photo if possible. Example: “iPhone 13, cracked screen, touch still works, Twin Falls.”

Is return shipping really free?

Yes. Return shipping is included on every completed repair, whether you mailed the device or dropped it off in person.

Twin Falls has repair shops — why use you?

How much does an iPhone screen repair cost from Twin Falls?

Can you replace a MacBook or laptop battery I mail from Twin Falls?

Yes — laptop batteries start from $69 and MacBook batteries vary by model, so text the exact model for a firm quote. Battery and screen jobs ship well and almost always beat buying a new machine. It comes back tested with free return shipping.
