# Idaho Mail-In Device Repair

HDR offers quote-first mail-in repair for Idaho customers who cannot drop off in Hailey. Useful for iPhone, Android, iPad/tablet, MacBook, laptop, console, and some computer problems.

## Process

Text first, get a quote path, pack the device, ship it, receive updates, approve repair, and get the device returned after testing.

## Contact

Text (208) 366-6111 before shipping anything.
