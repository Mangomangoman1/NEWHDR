# iPhone 16 Pro Max model — reference notes

This is a procedural visual study. The published exterior envelope is dimensioned; internal geometry is proportioned from public photographs. It is not an Apple CAD model, validated optical simulation, circuit schematic or repair procedure.

| Reference | Applied to the model | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Apple specifications and exterior photographs](https://support.apple.com/en-au/121032) | 77.6 × 163 × 8.25 mm enclosure; 1320:2868 screen aspect; titanium, matte rear glass, three-camera layout, controls and ports | Corner radii, button recesses and camera bump details are estimated from images. |
| [Apple internal and exploded views](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120821) | Relative locations of battery, logic board, camera, Taptic Engine, speakers, microphone, dock flex, antenna and cowlings | Hidden geometry and subcomponent dimensions are not supplied. The diagram is a rear view, so its left/right are mirrored in the model's front-facing coordinates. |
| [Apple camera procedure](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120824) | Camera carrier incorporates LiDAR and moves as one assembly; attachment points and flex tails | Glass curvature and coating thickness are authored shading choices. No claim of an exact seven-element telephoto optical prescription or internal tetraprism geometry. |
| [Apple battery procedure](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120828) | One shaped pouch and three adhesive strips | Foil, labels, seams and fold shapes are visual interpretations. |
| [Apple top-speaker procedure](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120825) | Separate top speaker and antenna unit | Models differ by market; this is a US-style exterior without a SIM tray. |
| [Apple Taptic Engine procedure](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/120829) | Sealed motor, cowling, flex and mounting hardware | Motor internals are left sealed rather than invented and exposed. |

Microvias, copper routing, solder arrays, weld patterns, coil turns, fastener microgeometry and manufacturing marks are illustrative. They are not exact component counts or recovered board schematics. The lock-screen artwork is authored for the animation. Parts separate in an artistic sequence while maintaining a thin assembled exterior.

The baked reflection environment uses six luminous panels. Geometry, textures and lighting are generated locally; the site does not download reference images or remote assets to render the phone. The more expensive transmission and shadow passes improve desktop inspection, while the existing adaptive resolution/shadow governor remains active for playback. Physical-handset performance still needs measurement on actual devices.
