# Hailey Device Repair: reinforce the search growth already working

## Executive Summary

- **Two phone guides drive most of the increase.** August 9–September 5 produced 223 clicks and 28,305 impressions, versus 89 and 8,443 during July 12–August 8: clicks +150.6%, impressions +235.2%. The black-screen and cracked-screen guides account for 74.0% of the increase in page-level impressions and 80.9% of the increase in page-level clicks.
- **Specific symptoms outperform broad repair terms.** Drop → black screen → still rings, hairline crack → glass versus display, and PS5 → no TV signal are demonstrated patterns. Preserve the successful pages and connect related diagnostics and services.
- **The iPhone service page needs indexing attention.** Direct URL Inspection said it was discovered but not indexed. Google's live test passed, and an indexing request was accepted. This is a practical commercial opportunity alongside informational growth.
- **Avoid premature expansion.** PS5 overheating has 1,143 impressions at position 6.53, but only 27 impressions have disclosed queries. Its June creation date also means it is not a newly published August article. New content should answer distinct observed intent, with original repair evidence.

## B. Positive trends and business relevance

**Drop-related black-screen searches are the strongest disclosed cluster.** “i dropped my phone and the screen is black but it still works” has 117 impressions, 2 clicks, position 6.56; the shorter “i dropped my phone and the screen is black” has 83 impressions at 7.41. Both had zero disclosed impressions in the preceding period. The existing guide directly satisfies this intent.

**Hairline cracks are a second demonstrated cluster.** The cracked-screen page has 25 disclosed queries containing “hairline,” totaling 271 impressions versus 37. The two leading variants have 48 and 47 impressions at positions 8.79 and 7.43. This supports a clearer section heading, not a competing hairline article.

**New guides gain exposure within their first two weeks.** Git records PS5 no-signal, laptop-hinge, and plugged-in-not-charging guides added August 23. By September 5 they have 738, 788, and 513 impressions respectively. Those are cumulative early-period totals, not verified 70–100-impression daily rates. No-signal and hinge rank much better than the charging guide (position 35.31).

**PS5 overheating is an older page gaining new exposure.** Git records creation June 13. All 1,143 impressions in the three-month export fall within the latest 28 days. Its disclosed queries include dust and fan symptoms, but are too sparse to explain most exposure or justify a standalone fan, hot-air, or shutdown article yet.

**Growth is increasingly international.** US impressions rose from 5,739 to 12,562 and clicks from 79 to 128. Non-US clicks rose from 10 to 95. Mobile impressions rose from 3,432 to 16,112; desktop from 4,915 to 11,580. Mobile accounts for about 63.8% of the property impression increase. Helpful informational traffic is valuable, but international readership is not equivalent to Idaho repair demand; do not broaden shipping promises to chase it.

The first two overlap successful existing answers; the latter lack sufficient disclosed query evidence. Recent September iPhone guides should be given time before adding more variants.

## F. CTR and snippets

The black-screen title stays unchanged because its ranking, clicks and CTR improved. No title or meta-description changes were implemented.

## Evidence limits

Property and page aggregations differ: the page-table impression increase is 19,878 and click increase 136, versus 19,862 and 134 at property level. Contribution shares above use the page-table denominator consistently. Average positions are exposure-weighted aggregates, not fixed ranks; zero prior impressions means no ranking comparison, not position zero.
