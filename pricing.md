# Hailey Device Repair Pricing

HDR uses quote-first repair pricing. Customers get a firm quote before approved work begins when the problem can be diagnosed.

## What affects price

Device model, part quality, availability, labor complexity, prior repair attempts, liquid damage, data risk, board damage, and whether multiple parts are failing.

## Best next step

Text (208) 450-1606 with the model, symptoms, what happened, and photos. HDR will say whether the repair is likely worth it before you spend money.
