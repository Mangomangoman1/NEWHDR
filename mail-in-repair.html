Questions

Mail-in FAQ

How does mail-in repair work? expand_more

Text or email us to describe your issue. We'll confirm the repair and give you a shipping address. Pack your device securely and ship it. We'll text you when it arrives, when the repair starts, and when we ship it back. Free return shipping included.

How long does the whole process take? expand_more

Most repairs are completed within 24 hours of receiving your device. With shipping each way, total turnaround is typically 3-5 business days depending on your location in Idaho. Boise/Nampa customers often get devices back in 2-3 days.

Is return shipping really free? expand_more

Yes. Return shipping via USPS Priority Mail is always free. You only pay for shipping your device to us. We can also provide a prepaid shipping label for an additional $8 if you'd prefer not to arrange your own shipping.

How much does mail-in repair usually cost? expand_more

Most common mail-in jobs fall into these ranges: phones $79-$279+, tablets $89-$249+, laptops $99-$349+, and game consoles $59-$149+. Exact price depends on your model, the part needed, and whether the damage is limited to one component or not. See the pricing section above for realistic ranges.

What should I text you to get a fast quote? expand_more

Send the exact model, what broke, whether it still powers on, and a photo if you can. Example: "iPhone 13, front glass shattered but touch still works. Boise. Need it back this week." That's enough for me to tell you whether it makes sense to ship and what price range you're probably in.

What if you can't fix my device? expand_more

Every repair starts with a thorough diagnosis. We'll explain what we found and give you a clear quote before any work begins. If the repair isn't feasible, we'll let you know honestly — and ship your device back at no charge. No hard feelings.

How should I pack my device? expand_more

Use a sturdy box with plenty of padding — bubble wrap, packing paper, or even crumpled newspaper works. Remove any cases or accessories. For phones, a small USPS Priority Mail box ($8.15 at any post office) is perfect. Power off the device before shipping. See our full packing guide above.

Do you repair devices from outside Idaho? expand_more

Currently we focus on Idaho to keep turnaround times fast. If you're in a neighboring state (Montana, Wyoming, Utah, Oregon, Washington, Nevada), text us and we can probably work something out. Shipping is just a day or two longer.

Is my data safe? Will you wipe my phone? expand_more

Never without asking you first. We don't wipe data as part of any standard repair. Screen replacements, battery swaps, charging port fixes — none of these require touching your data. If a repair does require a reset (rare), we'll contact you first and make sure you have a backup.

Can I use my own shipping label? expand_more

Absolutely. Ship via USPS, FedEx, or UPS — whatever's easiest for you. USPS Priority Mail is the cheapest option for most devices and arrives in 1 day from anywhere in Idaho. We can also email you a prepaid label for $8 if you'd rather skip the post office counter.

Do you offer a warranty on mail-in repairs? expand_more

Yes. All repairs come with a warranty covering parts and labor. If the same issue comes back, get in touch and we'll make it right. See our full warranty details.

What about water-damaged devices? expand_more

We can assess water damage by mail, but results depend on how long it's been wet and whether the device was powered on after exposure. Text us your situation before shipping and we'll give you an honest assessment of whether it's worth sending. See our water damage first-aid guide.

I'm a college student. Any discounts? expand_more

Yes. We offer student and military discounts on most repairs. Mention it when you text us. See details on our student & military page.

What's the cheapest way to ship my device? expand_more

For phones and small tablets: USPS Small Flat Rate Box ($8.15) — fits most phones wrapped in bubble wrap, arrives next day in Idaho. For laptops and consoles: USPS Medium Flat Rate Box ($16.10). You can also use any sturdy box you already have and ship Priority Mail by weight — often similarly priced.

Can you fix a cracked back glass on iPhone? expand_more

Yes. Back glass repair is one of our most common mail-in jobs, especially for iPhone 11 and newer models. Pricing varies by model — text us your exact iPhone model and we'll give you a quote in minutes. See iPhone repair details.

What if my device gets damaged during shipping? expand_more

This is extremely rare when devices are packed properly, but it's why we include detailed packing instructions. Use enough padding, use a sturdy box, and you'll be fine. USPS Priority Mail includes $100 of insurance by default. For higher-value devices, you can add extra coverage at the counter.

Do I need to include my passcode? expand_more

We need to be able to test the device after repair, so yes — include your passcode on a note inside the box (not written on the outside). We only use it to verify the repair worked. Your data stays private.

How do I pay for the repair? expand_more

We'll send you a payment link by text once the repair is done and tested. You can pay by card or Apple Pay. Payment happens after the repair is complete, not before — so you know it's actually fixed before you pay.
