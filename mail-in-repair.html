Packing

Four things, then the post office.

  1. Power it off and take off the case.
  2. Wrap it in two layers of bubble wrap, in a sturdy box with no room to slide.
  3. Add a note with your name, number and passcode.
  4. Text the tracking number once it's in the mail.
Box sizes and extras by device

Phones and tablets: a USPS Small Flat Rate box shipped Priority usually fits. Leave chargers and accessories at home.

Laptops: a box at least 2 inches bigger on every side, or a USPS Medium Flat Rate box. Put the charger in its own bag. A laptop bag alone isn't enough protection.

Consoles: the original box is ideal, otherwise a Medium Flat Rate box or any sturdy box by weight. Take out discs and cartridges, and wrap Joy-Cons separately.

Questions

Mail-in questions, answered.

How does mail-in repair work?

Text or email us to describe your issue. We'll confirm the repair and give you a shipping address. Pack your device securely and ship it. We'll text you when it arrives, when the repair starts, and when it's done. We send the invoice by text or email, and once it's paid we ship it back. Return shipping is included in the mail-in price.

How long does mail-in repair take?

Most repairs are done within 1-2 days of arriving on the bench, depending on the device and the job. Shipping time each way is on top of that, and we text you at every handoff so you always know where your device is.

Is return shipping included?

Yes. Tracked USPS Priority Mail return shipping is built into every mail-in price, anywhere in the lower 48. You only pay the postage to send your device to us.

How much does mail-in repair usually cost?

Most common mail-in jobs fall into these ranges: phones $85-$380+, tablets $165-$985+, laptops $185-$810+, and game consoles $60-$295+. Exact price depends on your model, the part needed, and whether the damage is limited to one component or not.

What should I text to get a fast quote?

Send the exact model, what broke, whether it still powers on, and a photo if you can. Example: iPhone 13, front glass shattered but touch still works. Denver. Need it back this week. That is enough for us to tell you whether it makes sense to ship and what price range you are probably in.

What if you can't fix my device?

Every repair starts with a thorough diagnosis. We'll explain what we found and give you a clear quote before any work begins. If the repair isn't feasible, we'll tell you honestly and ship your device back with return postage on us and no repair charge. If it needed a bench diagnosis (no-power, water damage or a mystery fault), the $60 bench fee still applies; when we can fix it, that fee is credited toward the repair.

How should I pack my device for shipping?

Use a sturdy box with plenty of padding — bubble wrap, packing paper, or even crumpled newspaper works. Remove any cases or accessories. For phones, you can use a small USPS Priority Mail box (free at any post office). Power off the device before shipping.

Do you take mail-in repairs from outside Idaho?

Yes. We take mail-in repairs from anywhere in the lower 48 states at the same published prices, with the same 40-day warranty. You pay the postage to us, and we pay it back.

Is there anything you won't take by mail?

TVs are the one thing we don't take. For anything else, text a photo and ask.

Is my data safe? Will you wipe my phone?

Never without asking you first. We don't wipe data as part of any standard repair. Screen replacements, battery swaps, charging port fixes — none of these require touching your data. If a repair does require a reset (rare), we'll contact you first.

Can I use my own shipping label?

Yes. You buy the postage to us, so ship via USPS, FedEx, or UPS, whatever's easiest. USPS Priority Mail is the simplest choice for most devices. Text us the tracking number once it's in the mail.

Do you offer a warranty on mail-in repairs?

Yes. Mail-in repairs get the same 40-day warranty on parts and labor as walk-in repairs, wherever you ship from. Warranty work ships the same way: you send it to us, and return shipping is on us.

What about water-damaged devices?

We can assess water damage by mail, but results depend on how long it's been wet and whether the device was powered on after exposure. Text us your situation before shipping for an honest assessment.

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes. We offer student and military discounts on most repairs. Mention it when you text us.

What's the cheapest way to ship my device?

For phones and small tablets, a USPS Small Flat Rate box is usually the easy pick. For laptops and consoles, a USPS Medium Flat Rate box or any sturdy box shipped Priority Mail by weight. The post office counter or usps.com shows current postage.

Can you fix cracked back glass on iPhone?

Yes. Back glass repair is one of our most common mail-in jobs, especially for iPhone 11 and newer models. Text us your exact iPhone model for a quote.

What if my device gets damaged during shipping?

This is extremely rare when devices are packed properly. USPS Priority Mail includes $100 of insurance by default. For higher-value devices, you can add extra coverage at the counter.

Do I need to include my passcode?

Yes — include your passcode on a note inside the box. We need it to test the device after repair. We only use it to verify the repair worked.

How do I pay for the repair?

When the repair is done and tested, we send an invoice by text or email. Once it's paid, your device ships back. Nothing is due before the repair is finished, so you know it's fixed before you pay.
