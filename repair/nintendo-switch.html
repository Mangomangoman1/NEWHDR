02 / By mail

Send it in, get it back fixed

  1. 01Text the model and what broke

    A photo of the damage helps. You get a firm quote back before anything ships.

  2. 02Pack it and send it

    Power it off, take off the case, wrap it well and ship it from anywhere in the lower 48 with USPS, UPS or FedEx. Postage to us is yours.

  3. 03Repaired on the bench

    You get a text when it arrives. Most repairs are finished in 1–2 days, depending on the job, and anything unexpected is texted to you before extra work.

  4. 04Pay, then it ships back

    The invoice comes by text or email. Once it is paid, it ships back tested and tracked, with return postage on us.
