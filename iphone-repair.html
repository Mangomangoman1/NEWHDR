iPhone FAQ

The questions people ask before handing over their phone.

How much does iPhone screen repair cost in Hailey?expand_more

iPhone screen repair starts at $109, but the exact quote depends on model, display type, frame condition, and part availability. Send the model and a photo of the damage first; I’ll quote the repair before parts are ordered or the phone is opened.

Will I lose photos, messages, or apps?expand_more

Normal screen, battery, camera, speaker, and charging-port repairs do not erase data. If the phone is water damaged, boot looping, or no-power, I treat data as the priority and explain the risk before attempting a repair path.

Do you use genuine Apple parts?expand_more

I quote parts honestly by model and availability. Many repairs use OEM-quality aftermarket parts chosen for fit, display quality, touch response, and value. If a part type has a tradeoff, I explain it before you approve the repair.

My iPhone got wet. What should I do first?expand_more

Power it off if you can, do not charge it, do not use rice, and text me as soon as possible. Liquid damage gets worse when current keeps moving through corrosion. A fast triage gives the phone and the data a better chance.

Can I mail in an iPhone from outside Hailey?expand_more

Yes. If you are in Idaho but not near Hailey, text the model and issue first. I’ll confirm whether the repair is a good mail-in candidate, send packing instructions, and keep updates in the text thread until it ships back.
