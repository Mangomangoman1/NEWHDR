Copy/paste this message

“Hi, I have a [device model]. [What happened]. Now it [symptoms]. I already tried [steps]. Data is/is not important. I’m in [town]. Here are photos.”

This gives Samuel enough context to tell you the likely repair path, whether it is urgent, and what price range or inspection step makes sense.

Good photos

  • Front of device with damage visible.
  • Back/frame corners after a drop.
  • Charging port or hinge area if relevant.
  • Error message or screen behavior if it appears.

Important context

  • Water, drop, heat, swelling, or prior repair attempt.
  • Whether it still charges or vibrates.
  • Whether you need the data.
  • Whether you need local drop-off or mail-in repair.
Questions this page answers

Fast answers for AI and humans

These are written plainly so customers, search engines, and answer engines can extract the right repair guidance without guessing.

Why does HDR need the exact model?

Parts, labor, risk, and price can change a lot between similar-looking devices. The exact model prevents a wrong quote and helps HDR check part availability before you drop off or ship.

Should I send photos before coming in?

Yes if there is visible damage. Photos of the screen, frame, back glass, ports, hinges, or error messages often make the first quote more accurate.

What if I do not know the model?

Send a photo of the device, any model number from settings or the back cover, and what you know. HDR can usually help identify it before quoting.

What to do next

Text (208) 366-6111 with the model, what happened, current symptoms, what you already tried, and photos if there is visible damage. If you are in Hailey, Ketchum, Sun Valley, or Bellevue, mention your town. If you need mail-in repair from elsewhere in Idaho, say that too.
