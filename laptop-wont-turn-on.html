First five minutes

Do this before you press power again.

A dead laptop can still be recoverable. The trick is knowing when a safe reset helps and when another power attempt can make the repair harder.

Stop if it is wet, hot, swollen, or smells burnt

Unplug it. Do not charge it. Do not use rice, heat, a hair dryer, or repeated power attempts. Put a swollen battery device on a hard surface.

Remove docks, hubs, drives, and extras

Disconnect monitors, printers, USB drives, docks, hubs, SD cards, and external gear. A bad accessory can keep a laptop from booting cleanly.

Try one safe reset, not ten

If there are no danger signs, one power-drain attempt is fine. Repeated cycling is not diagnosis, especially if your files matter.

Symptom decoder

Which kind of dead is it?

People say "won't turn on" for several different failures. These are the clues I use before I ever order parts.

Data note: If your files matter more than the laptop, say that first. Some repairs should be handled as data recovery before normal troubleshooting.

No lights at all

Could be outlet, charger, cable, USB-C/DC jack, battery protection, board short, or liquid damage.

Charging light only

Often battery, power button/top case, embedded controller, BIOS/firmware, or board-level power issue.

Fans or keyboard light, black screen

May not be no-power. Could be display, backlight, RAM, GPU, boot drive, or firmware.

Starts then shuts off

Shorted component, bad battery, thermal protection, board fault, or spill corrosion.

Works only plugged in

Battery failure, charging circuit issue, battery connector, or power management problem.

USB-C feels loose

Could be debris, worn cable, damaged port, USB-C board, or motherboard-side charging failure.

No-power laptop FAQ

Straight answers before you bring it in.

Why won't my laptop turn on even when it is plugged in?

It can be as simple as a charger or as serious as a board short. The main suspects are outlet, charger, cable, USB-C/DC port, battery, embedded controller, power button, liquid damage, or motherboard/logic board rails.

Should I keep pressing the power button?

No. One controlled reset attempt is enough if there are no hazard signs. Repeated power cycling is especially risky after liquid, heat, swelling, or burning smells.

My laptop has no lights at all. Is it the charger or motherboard?

Maybe either. No lights can point to charger/cable, port, battery protection, or board-level failure. Bring the charger so I can test the full path.

My keyboard lights up but the screen is black. Is that no power?

Usually not. That may be a display, backlight, RAM, GPU, firmware, or boot issue. It is still diagnosable, but the repair path is different from a totally dead laptop.

Can you fix a MacBook that won't turn on?

Yes. MacBook Air and MacBook Pro no-power work can involve USB-C charging, battery, liquid damage, top case, firmware, or logic board diagnosis. For newer MacBooks with soldered storage, getting the board alive can matter for data access.

Can you fix Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, and Surface laptops?

Yes. Windows laptop no-power cases often involve charger wattage, DC jack or USB-C port, battery, screen-vs-power confusion, motherboard faults, or firmware problems.

Can a bad battery stop a laptop from turning on?

Yes. Some laptops will run from charger with a bad battery, and some will not behave normally until the battery path is isolated. Swollen batteries should not be used or charged.

How do I know if the charging port is broken?

Common signs are a loose cable, charging only at an angle, no charger light, intermittent charging, warmth near the port, or visible debris/damage. Do not force the plug.

Can my files be saved from a dead laptop?

Often. A dead laptop does not always mean dead data. Recovery depends on drive health, encryption, model design, and whether storage is removable or soldered. Tell me if data matters first.

Will no-power repair erase my data?

Normal hardware diagnosis does not require erasing data. If any repair path risks data, I will tell you before proceeding.

How much does it cost to fix a laptop that won't turn on?

It depends on where power stops. Charger, battery, and port issues are usually simpler; board-level or liquid damage cases need diagnosis before a real quote. I give the quote before parts or repair work.

Should I bring or mail the charger?

Yes, if the problem involves power or charging. Charger behavior is part of the diagnosis, especially for USB-C laptops and high-wattage gaming laptops.

Do you offer no-power laptop repair by mail in Idaho?

Yes, when it makes sense for the device and symptom. Text first with the model and problem before shipping anything.

What if the laptop is not worth fixing?

I will say so. If the repair costs too much compared to replacement, we can focus on data recovery, migration, or replacement advice instead.
