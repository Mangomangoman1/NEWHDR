How to read these prices

  • They’re real menu prices, not bait. If hidden damage changes the job, you hear the new number before I touch anything — that’s the quote-first rule.
  • Parts tier: quality aftermarket or OEM service packs. Genuine manufacturer parts are available on most jobs and cost more — ask and I’ll quote both.
  • Mail-in prices include return shipping. You ship it to Hailey (or drop off locally); the tracked trip back to you is built into the mail-in column.
  • Diagnosis: text-photo quotes are free. Real bench diagnosis — no-power machines, water damage, mystery faults — is $45, credited toward the repair.
  • PC laptops: hardware repairs are quoted per job after diagnosis (models vary too much for a fixed menu). The flat-rate services above cover the common software and thermal work.
  • Prices move with the parts market. This table is maintained by hand and dated — if a part’s cost drops, so does the price.

Pricing questions

Are these the final prices?

These are real menu prices for standard repairs with quality aftermarket or OEM-service-pack parts. Text your model and photos first — if hidden damage changes the job, you hear the real number before any work starts, never after.

How much does iPhone screen repair cost in Hailey?

It depends on the model: iPhone SE and 8-series screens start around $100–$115, iPhone 11 is $120, iPhone 13 is $165, iPhone 15 is $185, and newer Pro Max panels run up to $275. Every model is listed in the table above.

Do prices include tax?

Idaho does not tax repair labor when it’s itemized separately from parts — so every HDR invoice itemizes parts and labor, and you pay 6% only on the parts line. Flat-rate services are labor-only, so there’s no sales tax on them at all.

What does mail-in repair cost?

Every row shows a mail-in price — it’s the local price plus tracked return shipping back to you anywhere in Idaho. You pay outbound shipping to Hailey; the return trip is built into the mail-in price.

Can I get genuine Apple or Samsung parts?

Yes. Listed prices assume quality aftermarket or OEM service-pack parts. Genuine manufacturer parts typically cost more — text your model and ask for the genuine-part quote and I’ll price both options.

Is there a diagnostic fee?

Text-photo quotes are free. If a device needs real bench time to diagnose — no-power laptops, water damage, mystery faults — the bench fee is $45 and it’s credited toward the repair if you go ahead.
