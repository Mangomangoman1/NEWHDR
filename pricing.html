Pricing FAQ

Are these the exact prices I'll pay? expand_more

These are starting-at prices. The exact cost depends on your specific device model and the extent of the damage. For example, an iPhone 12 screen costs less than an iPhone 15 Pro Max screen. You'll always get a firm quote before any work begins — no surprises.

Why are your prices lower than Apple or Samsung? expand_more

We use high-quality aftermarket parts instead of OEM, and we don't carry the overhead of a retail storefront. Lower costs mean lower prices — but same quality. Every part we install is thoroughly tested and backed by our 90-day warranty.

Do you charge for looking at my device? expand_more

Never. Diagnosis is always free — phones, laptops, tablets, consoles, everything. We'll tell you what's wrong, give you a price, and you decide. Walk away if you want — no pressure, no charge.

What payment methods do you accept? expand_more

Cash, Venmo, Zelle, Apple Pay, and most major credit/debit cards. For mail-in repairs, we'll send a payment link via text once the repair is complete — you pay before we ship.

Is there a trip fee for curbside? expand_more

No trip fees in the Wood River Valley (Bellevue, Hailey, Ketchum, Sun Valley). We come to you — your home, office, or wherever is convenient. Outside the Valley, text us and we'll figure something out.
