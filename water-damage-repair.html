First 60 Minutes

The rescue checklist

Tap each step as you complete it. Every step you check buys your device more time.

  • check
    Power it off immediately
    Don't check if it still works. Every second powered on increases short-circuit risk. Hold the power button and shut it down.
  • check
    Don't charge it
    Electricity + water = corrosion. Don't plug it in to "test." If it's already charging, unplug it safely without touching wet surfaces.
  • check
    Skip the rice
    Rice doesn't absorb moisture from inside sealed devices. It can leave starch dust in ports. Silica gel is slightly better, but not a fix.
  • check
    Dry the outside gently
    Use a lint-free cloth to absorb visible water from the exterior, ports, and buttons. Be gentle — don't push water deeper inside.
  • check
    Keep it face-down
    Place the device face-down on a dry cloth so gravity helps moisture move away from internal components rather than pooling inside.
  • check
    Text me now
    The sooner I open and dry it properly, the higher the recovery chance. Corrosion starts within hours. Don't wait until tomorrow.

Myth Busting

What not to do

These "hacks" circulate online. They usually make things worse.

grain
Put it in rice
Rice doesn't reach moisture inside sealed devices. It can introduce starch dust into ports and cracks, creating new problems.
air
Use a hair dryer
Heat damages adhesives, warps components, and can push water deeper. Hot air also creates condensation inside when it cools.
vibration
Shake it hard
Vigorous shaking can drive water further into the logic board and connectors. Gentle tapping face-down is the safer approach.
power
Charge it to "test"
This is the single most damaging thing you can do. Electricity accelerates corrosion and can permanently short components.
By Device

What recovery looks like

Different devices, different risks, different outcomes. Here's what to expect.

smartphone

Phone

Highest recovery rate if fast. Modern phones are densely packed, so corrosion spreads quickly — but they're also the easiest to open and dry properly.

~70-85% recoverable Same-day assessment
computer

Laptop

Keyboard spills are common and often recoverable. The key is immediate power-off and not moving the laptop around, which spreads liquid across the motherboard.

~60-75% recoverable Data usually saveable
tablet_android

Tablet

Similar to phones but with larger screens that trap moisture. iPads in particular have tight seals that can hold water against the board for days.

~65-80% recoverable Screen often affected
