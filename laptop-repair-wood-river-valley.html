Repairs

What I fix

Laptops die in specific ways. Here's what each type looks like — and what the repair involves.

MacBook

  • Screen replacement — Retina, non-Retina, cracked, no backlight, lines
  • Battery replacement — won't hold charge, swollen, shuts off early
  • MagSafe / USB-C charging — won't charge, port debris, intermittent
  • Liquid / spill damage — coffee, water, wine — clean and assess
  • Won't power on — board-level diagnostic, no boot, black screen
  • SSD upgrade — faster storage, more space, better performance
  • Keyboard damage — spill damage, key repair, full replacement

Windows Laptops

  • Screen replacement — cracked, dead pixels, no backlight, flickering
  • Battery replacement — won't hold charge, won't recognize charger
  • DC jack / charging connector — loose port, no charge, broken pin
  • Liquid / spill damage — power off, clean board, assess damage
  • SSD upgrade / RAM — slow laptop made fast again
  • Virus & malware removal — pop-ups, ransomware, fake alerts
  • Overheating / fan noise — thermal cleanup, paste, fan replacement

Chromebook & Other

  • Screen replacement — cracked display, no image, backlight failure
  • Charging port — USB-C port damage, won't charge
  • Won't power on — battery, charging, or board diagnostic
  • Keyboard / touchpad — spill damage, stuck keys
  • Small business computer setup — new machine, printer, Wi-Fi, backup
  • Startup & performance — slow boot, crashes, cleanup
  • Data recovery — files off a dead or damaged drive
Getting a quote

What to text for a laptop quote

A good first text saves two rounds of back-and-forth and gets you a quote faster. Four things matter most.

1

Brand, model, year

MacBook Pro 14" M2 2023, Dell XPS 15, HP Spectre, ASUS Chromebook. The exact model changes which parts are needed and what they cost.

2

What happened

Drop, spill, sudden shutdown, gradual slowdown. When it happened matters — a spill from two hours ago is different from a spill from two weeks ago.

3

What it does now

Any screen image, charging light, fan spin, beeps, or sounds when you press power. Silence and black screen together mean something different than silence alone.

4

Whether files matter

If there are irreplaceable files on the drive, say so. It changes the repair priority order — data preservation comes before diagnosis on risky drives.

Symptom guide

What to do based on your symptom

Screen broken

Cracked or dead display

Text the model and a photo of the damage. Screen repairs are typically same-day when parts are staged. MacBook Retina and touchscreen Windows panels cost more than standard displays — the quote depends on the exact panel.

Won't power on

No response, no lights, no fan

Don't cycle the charger repeatedly if there's liquid history. Text the model, whether you hear anything when you press power, and whether there's any charging light. A complete no-power situation with liquid history is urgent.

Slow / overheating

Performance or heat problem

A 5–7 year old laptop running hot and slow is often a thermal problem: clogged vents, degraded paste, or a failing fan. Combined with an SSD upgrade if the drive is old, many machines are faster than new. Text the model and age.

Liquid damage

Spill — coffee, water, anything

Power it off immediately. Do not plug it in. Bring it in as fast as possible — corrosion starts within hours and gets worse every day. Text me on the way so the bench is ready. Speed matters more than anything else here.

Honest assessment

When to repair, when to replace

Not every repair is worth it. Here's how to think about it before you drive anywhere.

Repair makes sense

Drive it in when…

  • The screen broke but everything else works
  • Battery won't hold charge but the machine is fine otherwise
  • Charging port is damaged or intermittent
  • Liquid spill just happened — get it in fast
  • Slow laptop is otherwise in good shape
  • Files on the drive are irreplaceable
Mail it if…

Mail-in is a good option when…

  • You're a seasonal resident or vacation visitor
  • Driving to Hailey doesn't fit your week
  • You're elsewhere in Idaho and the drive is long
  • Remote workers who can't be without the laptop — we'll coordinate timing
Consider replacement when…

Replacement may be smarter if…

  • The machine is 8+ years old with multiple failures
  • Repair cost approaches 60–70% of a comparable replacement
  • Manufacturer parts are discontinued and unavailable
  • The laptop no longer runs the software you need
Laptop FAQ

Questions about laptop repair in the Wood River Valley

Do you repair MacBooks in Hailey, Idaho?

Yes. MacBook screen replacement, battery replacement, MagSafe and USB-C charging repair, liquid damage, keyboard issues, and no-power diagnostics are all handled from Hailey. You don't need to ship to Apple or drive to Boise.

Do I need to go to the Apple Store for a MacBook repair?

Not for most repairs. Screen replacement, battery replacement, charging port repair, and liquid damage cleanup don't require Apple authorization. If a repair requires an Apple tool or part I can't source, I'll tell you upfront before any work starts.

How much does MacBook screen repair cost in Hailey?

MacBook screen repair cost depends on the model and display type. Retina displays cost more than older non-Retina panels — and the newer the MacBook, the more model-specific the screen assembly. Text the exact model (for example, MacBook Pro 14-inch M2 2023) and a photo of the damage for a specific quote before anything is opened.

How much does laptop battery replacement cost?

Laptop battery replacement starts around $89 for most Windows laptops and varies for MacBooks by model. Text the brand, model, and year. If the battery is visibly swollen, don't charge or compress the laptop — bring it in as soon as possible.

My laptop won't turn on at all. What should I do?

Don't charge it if there's any liquid history. Text the model, what happened before it went dark, whether you hear any fan spin or see any lights when you press power, and whether files matter. That information changes the diagnosis path significantly. Silence plus a black screen with liquid history is an urgent situation — don't let it sit.

Can you fix a laptop with water or coffee damage?

Yes, but speed matters more than anything else. Power it off immediately, don't plug it in, and get it to me as fast as possible. Liquid damage spreads through corrosion over hours and days — the sooner the board is cleaned, the better the odds for both the laptop and the files. Text me on the way so the bench is ready.

Is it worth repairing a slow or old laptop?

Usually worth a quote. An SSD upgrade, malware removal, thermal cleanup (cleaning the vents and replacing the thermal paste), or battery replacement can make a 5–7 year old machine run significantly better. If repair cost approaches replacement value, I'll say so before any work starts.

How long does laptop repair take?

Most screen replacements and battery swaps are same-day or next-day when parts are in stock. Liquid damage takes longer — usually 2–4 days for cleaning, drying, and testing. Diagnostics are free and happen before any repair decision is made, so you're not paying for surprises.

Can I get same-day laptop repair if I'm coming from Ketchum or Sun Valley?

Yes, if the part is in stock. Text first before making the 15–20 minute drive from Ketchum. I'll stage the part on the bench so the repair is ready to start when you arrive. Most screen and battery jobs are finished while you ski, grab a coffee, or run errands in Hailey.

Do you fix Chromebooks?

Yes. Chromebook screen replacement, charging port repair, and power issues are common repairs. If the Chromebook is school-issued, check your school district's repair policy first — but third-party repair is usually faster and less expensive than school replacement. Text the model for a quote.
