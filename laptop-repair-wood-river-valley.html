For Hailey, Ketchum, Sun Valley, and Bellevue laptop problems, the right first move depends on the symptom: no power, slow performance, charging failure, spill, overheating, malware, or data risk. HDR can triage Windows laptops, Chromebooks, MacBooks, and small-business computers.

No power / no charge

Stop cycling chargers if the laptop smells hot, clicked off suddenly, or has liquid history. Text the model, charger behavior, light behavior, and whether files matter.

Slow laptop

Slow does not always mean replace. SSD upgrades, cleanup, malware removal, startup fixes, or thermal cleanup can make a machine useful again.

Small business help

HDR can help with practical computer support: setup, printer/Wi-Fi issues, malware cleanup, backup checks, and plain troubleshooting for local owners.

Repair or replace a laptop?

Repair is usually worth quoting when the laptop still fits your work, the screen/drive/battery/charging repair is isolated, or important files are at risk. Replacement is cleaner when the machine is very old, unsupported, physically damaged in several places, or repair cost approaches replacement value.

Can HDR fix laptops in Hailey?

Yes. HDR handles many Windows laptop, Chromebook, MacBook, charging, battery, slow performance, malware, SSD, no-power, and setup issues from Hailey, Idaho.

Should I replace a slow laptop?

Not automatically. If the laptop is not too old, an SSD upgrade, cleanup, malware removal, thermal service, or battery replacement can be cheaper than replacement.

What should I text for a laptop quote?

Send the laptop brand/model, what changed, whether it charges, any lights or fan behavior, what you already tried, and whether files matter.
