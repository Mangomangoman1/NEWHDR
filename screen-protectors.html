FAQ

Screen protector questions

The answers I give most often — so you don't have to wonder.

iPhone tempered glass screen protector installation starts at $25 including the protector and professional install. Android tempered glass starts at $25. iPad and tablet screen protectors start at $35. Premium options like privacy glass or matte anti-glare start at $35. The price includes the protector, professional installation, and a bubble-free guarantee.
Most screen protector installations take 10-15 minutes. I clean the screen under magnification, align the protector precisely, and apply it bubble-free. You can wait in the shop or run a quick errand in downtown Hailey. Same-day walk-ins are always welcome.
I stock tempered glass screen protectors for all current and recent iPhone models, most Samsung Galaxy and Pixel models, and common iPad sizes. If I don't have your exact model in stock, I can order it with next-day delivery or you can bring your own protector and I'll install it for $10.
Every installation comes with a bubble-free guarantee. If any bubbles appear within 48 hours, come back and I'll re-install it for free. I use a dust-free application process under magnification to prevent bubbles at the source. Dust is the main cause of bubbles, and I eliminate it before applying.
Tempered glass is 5-7 times stronger than plastic film, feels smoother to the touch, and protects against drops and impacts far better. Plastic film mainly prevents scratches. Tempered glass also maintains screen clarity and touch sensitivity better. For most people, tempered glass is worth the small extra cost.
Yes. Mail-in screen protector installation is available statewide across Idaho. Ship your device to Hailey Device Repair, and I'll install the protector the same day it arrives. Total turnaround including shipping is typically 2-3 days. The protector and installation are included in the quoted price.
