Diagnose Your Battery

What's your battery doing?

Tap the symptom that matches your device. I'll tell you what it means and what to do next.

battery_alert
Dies way too fast

Phone at 40% one minute, dead the next. Barely makes it to lunch.

This usually means: Your battery's maximum capacity has dropped below 80%. It's the most common battery issue and a straightforward replacement. Most iPhones and Androids get a full day of use again after a new cell is installed.
battery_charging_full
Won't hold a charge

Shows 100% but shuts off when unplugged. Or charges to a random percentage and stops.

This usually means: A failing cell or voltage regulator issue. The battery can't maintain stable voltage output. I test the charging circuit and replace the battery if it's the culprit — usually a 20-40 minute fix.
warning
Swollen or hot

Screen lifting, back bulging, or device gets uncomfortably warm during normal use.

Stop using it now. A swollen lithium-ion battery is a fire hazard. Power it down, don't charge it, and bring it in immediately. I can safely remove and replace it the same day. This is urgent — don't wait.
ac_unit
Random shutdowns

Device powers off in cold weather, during calls, or at seemingly random times.

This usually means: Your battery can't deliver peak power when the system demands it. Apple calls this "Peak Performance Capability" — when it fails, random shutdowns happen. A battery replacement restores stable power delivery.
