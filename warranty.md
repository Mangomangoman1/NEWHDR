# Hailey Device Repair Warranty

Most covered HDR repairs include a 40-day warranty on the repaired part and labor. The warranty is meant to cover defects or problems with the specific repair, not new damage or unrelated failures.

## Usually not covered

New drops, liquid exposure, pressure damage, customer-supplied parts unless explicitly accepted, unrelated board failures, software/account issues, and damage caused after pickup.

## Contact

If the same issue returns, text 208-450-1606 with the device model, repair date, and what changed.
