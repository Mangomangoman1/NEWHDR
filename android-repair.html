The exact model changes everything.

Android repair is not one price sheet. A Galaxy A screen, a Galaxy S Ultra OLED, a Pixel battery, and a foldable hinge are different jobs.

What to text for a useful quote

Send the phone model, what happened, what still works, whether data matters, and clear photos of the front, back, and charging port.

  • Model: Galaxy S24 Ultra, Pixel 8, Moto G Power, or whatever appears in Settings.
  • Symptom: cracked glass, black screen, touch failure, slow charging, swelling, water, camera, speaker, mic.
  • Photos: lock screen if visible, crack close-up, back cover, and port if charging is the issue.
If the screen is black but the phone ringsThat often points toward display damage, not a dead phone. Tell me whether touch still responds.
If it got wetPower it off and do not charge it. Fast cleaning matters more than rice or waiting overnight.
If the port is looseIt may be lint, port wear, or board damage. A port photo and cable test narrows it down quickly.

Three checks before you replace it.

A lot of Android phones look dead when one part is blocking the whole experience. These checks help separate a repairable phone from a money pit.

Does it still ring or vibrate?

If yes, the board may still be alive and a screen repair may recover normal use.

Does charging behave differently by cable?

That points toward port debris, cable quality, battery condition, or USB-C port damage.

Do photos and data matter?

Say that first. Data-first handling changes the safest bench move, especially after water or impact.

Android repair questions.

Short answers first. If your model is unusual, text it over and I will narrow it down.

How much does Samsung Galaxy screen repair cost?

It depends on the exact Galaxy model and whether it uses an OLED or LCD panel. Some start around $69, while newer flagship OLED parts can cost much more.

Can you fix Google Pixel phones?

Yes. I work on Pixel screens, batteries, charging issues, cameras, and other common failures. Text the model before ordering parts.

Can you fix Samsung Z Flip or Z Fold phones?

Yes, but foldables need a careful quote because the display and hinge assemblies are expensive and model-specific.

Will I lose my photos?

Normal screen, battery, and charging repairs do not erase data. If data is the main concern, tell me before repair starts so the bench plan stays data-first.

What should I do if it got wet?

Turn it off, do not charge it, and text right away. The sooner corrosion is handled, the better the odds for the phone and the data.
