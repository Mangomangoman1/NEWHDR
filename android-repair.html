Know Before You Go

Samsung AMOLED vs. Standard LCD

Android screen prices vary a lot — here's why.

contrast

AMOLED Screens

Samsung Galaxy S and Note series use AMOLED — deeper blacks, vibrant colors, but pricier to replace. Expect $100–$200+ for flagship models.

savings

LCD Screens

Budget Samsungs (A series), most Pixels, Motorola, and OnePlus use LCD — still great quality and much cheaper to replace. Often $69–$99.

help

Not Sure?

Just text me your model number and I'll tell you exactly what screen type you have and what it'll cost. No googling required.

Android FAQ

Questions about Android repair

How much does Samsung Galaxy screen repair cost? expand_more

Samsung Galaxy screen replacement starts at $69 for LCD models (A series, older S models). Flagship AMOLED screens (S24, S25, Note) cost more — typically $120–$200+ depending on model. I'll always quote you the exact price for your specific phone before starting.

Can you fix Google Pixel phones? expand_more

Yes. I repair all Google Pixel models from Pixel 3 through the latest Pixel 9 Pro series. Common repairs: screen replacement, battery swap, charging port, and camera module. Pixel screens are generally more affordable than Samsung flagships.

Can you fix a cracked Samsung Z Flip or Z Fold? expand_more

Yes, I repair Samsung foldables. These are more complex (and more expensive) due to the folding mechanism, but I can handle screen replacement, hinge issues, and battery swaps. Text me your model for an exact quote — pricing varies significantly by model and damage.

Do you repair Android tablets too? expand_more

Absolutely. Samsung Galaxy Tab, Lenovo Tab, and other Android tablets — screen replacements, battery swaps, and charging port fixes are all on the menu. Same process: text me, get a quote, drop off or ship.

Will I lose my photos and data? expand_more

No. Hardware repairs like screen replacement, battery swap, and port repair don't touch your data. Everything stays exactly as it is. In the rare case a factory reset is needed, I'll always warn you first so you can back up to Google Drive.

My Android phone got wet — what do I do? expand_more

Turn it off immediately. Don't charge it, don't blow-dry it, and definitely don't put it in rice (that's a myth). Text me at (208) 366-6111 right away — the faster I can open it and dry the internals properly, the better your chances. Water damage repair starts at $49.
