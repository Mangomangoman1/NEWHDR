What to text me so I can quote it fast

badge

Exact model

“iPhone 14” or “MacBook Air M1” is good. “Phone” or “laptop” is not.

heart_broken

What actually broke

Cracked screen, battery issue, no charge, bad keyboard, back glass, water exposure — be specific.

Boise FAQ

Questions Boise-area customers usually ask first

Is mail-in device repair worth it from Boise?expand_more

Often yes. Many Boise-area customers use this when they want clear pricing, fast communication, and a simple statewide mail-in process.

How long does shipping from Boise to Hailey take?expand_more

USPS Priority Mail from Boise and much of the Treasure Valley often reaches Hailey the next day. Many Boise-area repairs are back in the customer’s hands in 2–4 total days depending on the device and repair.

Is this worth it from Meridian or Nampa too?expand_more

Yes. Meridian and Nampa ship just as cleanly into the same process: text the issue, get a clear range, and send it in when you’re ready.

Can I mail in an iPhone from Boise for screen repair?expand_more

Yes. Cracked iPhone screens, back glass, battery issues, and charging-port problems are some of the most common Boise-area mail-in repairs. See the iPhone mail-in page.

Can I mail in a MacBook from Boise?expand_more

Yes. Battery replacement, display issues, charging problems, and keyboard or trackpad problems are all common MacBook mail-in jobs from Boise and the Treasure Valley. See the MacBook mail-in page.

Do I need to drive to Hailey first?expand_more

No. Boise-area customers can start by text, get an estimate, and decide on the repair without driving to Hailey first.

What should I text to get a quote?expand_more

Text the exact model, what broke, whether it still powers on or works, and a photo if possible. Example: “iPhone 14, front glass shattered, touch still works, Meridian.”

Is return shipping really free?expand_more

Yes. Return shipping is included on every completed repair. You only pay to get the device to Hailey.

Is this better than manufacturer mail-in?expand_more

Sometimes. The main difference is the communication: you deal directly with me, get updates by text, and can usually follow the turnaround more easily. Manufacturer mail-in can still make sense in some cases.

What if I’m in Caldwell, Eagle, Kuna, or Star instead of Boise?expand_more

This page covers the wider Treasure Valley, not just Boise. Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Eagle, Kuna, Star, and Garden City all use the same mail-in process.
