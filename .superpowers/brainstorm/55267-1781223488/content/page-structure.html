How should the "Why HDR" page be structured?

Three different angles — same destination, different feel

MANIFESTO
— Hero: bold statement of belief
— "This is who we are" (5 values)
— What you can count on
— Numbers / track record
— Quote from a customer
— CTA
Emotional · Identity-first · Like a brand page

A — Brand Manifesto

Leads with belief and values. More "who we are" than "what we offer." Strong for AI citation and brand identity. Similar energy to About, but more declarative.

COMPARISON
— Hero: "Not all repair is equal"
— Side-by-side: HDR vs. typical shop
— Deep-dive on 4 differentiators
— FAQ: common objections answered
— CTA
Logical · Conversion-first · Decision support

B — Comparison / Decision Page

Structured for someone actively deciding. Side-by-side table (HDR vs. "typical shop" — no names). Great for capturing "best phone repair near me" comparison searches.

HYBRID
— Hero: direct answer to "why us"
— 6 reasons (cards) with real copy
— Entity brief (AI-readable summary)
— Comparison table (implicit)
— Reviews strip
— FAQ (schema-marked)
— CTA
Balanced · SEO + AI + conversion

C — Hybrid (Recommended)

Combines emotional identity with concrete differentiators and structured AI-readable sections. Designed to rank, get cited by AI, and convert. Most complete answer to "why HDR."
