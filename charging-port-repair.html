Diagnose the Problem

What's your charging port doing?

Tap the symptom that matches your device. I'll tell you what it means and whether it's a quick fix.

power_off
Won't charge at all

Cable plugged in but no charging indicator. Device stays dead no matter how long it's connected.

This usually means: A completely failed port, broken internal pin, or detached solder joint. Sometimes it's debris packed so tight the cable can't reach the contacts. I can diagnose this in minutes and most repairs are same-day.
cable
Cable is loose or falls out

Cable doesn't "click" in anymore. Wiggles around, falls out with the slightest movement.

This usually means: Worn or bent retention clips inside the port, or the port has physically separated from the motherboard anchor. This is a very common issue and usually requires a port replacement — 30-60 minutes for most phones.
battery_charging_full
Charges only at certain angles

You have to hold the cable "just right" or prop it up with a book to get it to charge.

This usually means: Bent or partially detached pins inside the port. The cable is making intermittent contact. This will get worse until it stops working entirely. Best to fix it now before it damages the charging IC on the motherboard.
snooze
Charges very slowly

Takes hours to reach 50%. Used to fast-charge, now crawls. Or charges fine on wireless but not cable.

This usually means: Corroded or partially blocked pins reducing the connection quality. If wireless charging works but cable doesn't, the port is almost certainly the culprit. A cleaning or replacement restores full charging speed.
