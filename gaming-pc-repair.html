Component Diagnostic

What's failing inside your rig?

Tap the component that matches your symptoms. I'll tell you what it usually means and what to text me.

GPU / Graphics

Artifacts on screen, black screens during games, fans spinning at 100%, or no display at all.

This usually means: GPU overheating, failing VRMs, or a dead card. I test thermals under load, check PCIe power delivery, and re-paste if temps are high. If the card is dead, I can source a replacement and install it.
CPU / Processor

System freezes, stuttering, high temps at idle, or BIOS warning beeps on boot.

This usually means: Dried thermal paste, loose cooler mount, or insufficient power delivery. I reseat the cooler, apply fresh thermal compound, and validate voltages. If the CPU itself is failing, I help you pick a compatible replacement.
RAM / Memory

Random crashes, blue screens with memory-related codes, or games closing to desktop.

This usually means: Bad RAM stick, incompatible XMP profile, or loose DIMM seating. I run MemTest86, reseat modules, and test each stick individually. If you need more RAM, I install matched kits and verify stability.
PSU / Power

PC shuts off under load, won't power on, burning smell, or coil whine under the GPU.

This usually means: Underpowered or failing PSU. I test 12V rail stability under load and calculate your system's actual wattage draw. If the PSU is the culprit, I recommend a reliable replacement with proper headroom.
Cooling / Airflow

Loud fans, high temps, thermal throttling, or dust caked inside the case.

This usually means: Clogged filters, dead fans, or dried thermal paste. I deep-clean every component, replace thermal paste on CPU and GPU, validate fan curves, and check case airflow direction. Most gaming PCs run 10-20°C cooler after a proper cleaning.
Storage / SSD

Slow boot times, games taking forever to load, or "disk at 100%" in Task Manager.

This usually means: Full or failing drive, or an old HDD bottlenecking the system. I clone drives to faster NVMe SSDs, clean up storage, and validate SMART health. An SSD upgrade is the single biggest perceptible speed boost for most PCs.
