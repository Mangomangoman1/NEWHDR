Common signs

What virus problems usually look like

Half the time people say “my computer is just old” and it turns out the browser is hijacked, some garbage app is auto-launching, or fake security junk is lighting the machine on fire.

warning

What I do

I check the system for malware, suspicious startup junk, browser hijackers, adware, fake security tools, and the other garbage that makes a computer feel sketchy or unusable.

Then I clean it up, remove what shouldn’t be there, and make sure the machine is stable again. If the problem turns out to be something else, like a failing drive, bad RAM, or a Windows issue, I’ll tell you that too. I’m not going to call everything a virus just to sell a cleanup.

Plain-English answer
Do I need a new computer?

Maybe not. A lot of “dead” or “ancient” computers are just buried under junk, malware, or terrible startup clutter. Sometimes a cleanup is enough.

Can this be remote?

Sometimes, yeah. If the machine still boots and gets online, remote cleanup may work. If it’s worse than that, it needs to come in.
