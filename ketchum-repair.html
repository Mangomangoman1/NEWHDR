Mountain Life & Devices

The Wood River Valley breaks devices in specific ways

Years of north-valley repairs show clear patterns by season. Here’s what shows up on the bench — and what I can usually fix the same day you text.

Ski & winter season

Cold air shrinks lithium capacity — a phone that lasted all day in October dies at the chairlift by January. Hard-pack and lodge floor drops crack screens at odd angles. Goggle condensation and temperature swings work their way into charging ports.

Most common: battery swaps, screen cracks, charging port clogs. Most done same-day.

Summer on the water & trail

Big Wood River floats are the #1 source of water damage all summer. Mountain biking and trail running drops crack screens in ways that differ from pavement — sometimes the damage is deeper than it looks. Text a photo first; I’ll tell you honestly what’s salvageable.

Most common: water damage triage, cracked screens, heat-stressed batteries.

Visitor repairs on a deadline

If you’re visiting Sun Valley and broke something — text the model and when you fly out of SUN. I’ll tell you honestly whether same-day is doable before your flight. Friedman Memorial Airport is in Hailey, so the repair bench is literally on the way to your gate.

Key detail: text the moment it breaks — same-day needs a full day’s head start.

Ketchum & Sun Valley FAQ

Questions north-valley customers ask first

How far is the bench from Ketchum?

About 12 miles — 15–20 minutes south on ID-75 to Hailey. From Sun Valley Village or Elkhorn, add about five minutes. It’s the closest real repair bench in the valley.

Can my phone be fixed the same day?

Usually, yes. Text the exact model and problem first; if the part is on the shelf or can be staged, most screens and batteries take under an hour on the bench. The trick is texting before you drive down.

Do you come up to Ketchum or Sun Valley?

Drop-off in Hailey is the standard path — most Ketchum customers fold it into a trip they were already making. For some repairs a valley meetup can be coordinated; ask by text.

I’m visiting Sun Valley and broke my phone. Can you fix it before I leave?

Often, yes. Text the model and a photo as soon as it breaks. If the part’s available, the repair usually happens same-day or next-day — and Friedman Memorial Airport is in Hailey, so some visitors drop the phone on the way to their flight and have it shipped home.

Should I just take it to the Apple Store in Boise?

If AppleCare+ covers the damage, Apple can genuinely be the better deal — say so in your text and you’ll get an honest answer. Without coverage, that round trip is about 300 miles. Most out-of-warranty screens, batteries, and ports are simpler to handle 20 minutes away.

What’s the warranty?

Every completed repair carries a 40-day warranty on parts and labor. Full terms here.

What should I text to get a quote?

The exact model, what broke, whether it still works, and a photo. Example: “iPhone 14 Pro, cracked screen, touch still works, Ketchum.”

What about East Fork, Gimlet, or Bellevue?

The whole Wood River Valley works the same way: text first, part staged, drop off in Hailey, usually fixed the same day.

Is there a student or military discount?

Yes — 15% off any repair with a student ID or military/veteran ID. Details here.

My iPhone battery dies faster when skiing — can you replace it?

Yes, and it's one of the most common winter jobs from north-valley customers. Lithium batteries lose significant capacity below freezing — a battery at 80% health in October can feel like 50% on a cold January chairlift. An iPhone battery swap usually takes under an hour. Text the model and I'll confirm the part is on the shelf.

I dropped my phone on the ski run. What should I do?

Text me a photo as soon as you're off the mountain. Cracked glass that's still touch-responsive is usually a straightforward same-day screen repair — if the part is on the shelf it's typically done in under an hour. If the phone took a hard hit and is behaving oddly, describe the symptoms and you'll get an honest read before you drive down.

My MacBook got liquid on it at the lodge. Can it be saved?

Maybe — it depends on what spilled, how much, and how fast you powered it off. Golden rule: shut it down immediately, do not plug it in, and let it air dry upside-down. Then text me the model and what happened. Hot cocoa and coffee are rough on logic boards; water and beer are a bit more forgiving. I'll tell you honestly what the odds look like before you make the drive.

Do you repair MacBooks and laptops for Ketchum and Sun Valley residents?

Yes — MacBook battery replacement, screen repair, keyboard issues, and liquid damage are all common bench jobs for north-valley customers. Many Ketchum remote workers fold the drop-off into their regular grocery run down-valley. Text the model and the problem; if the part is available or can be staged, turnaround is usually same-day. See the MacBook repair page for details, or the Wood River Valley laptop repair page for local context.

Can I drop off my device and run Hailey errands while it's being fixed?

Yes — that's exactly how most Ketchum customers do it. Text from home the night before or that morning, confirm the part and the quote, drop off in Hailey, hit Albertsons or grab lunch, and pick up a fixed device on the way back north. Most screens and batteries are done within the hour.
