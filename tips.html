What should I do first if a device just got damaged?

Stop testing it if there is water, swelling, heat, smoke, no power, or important data involved. Power down when safe, avoid charging, take photos, and text the model, what happened, current symptoms, and what data matters.

Are these tips a replacement for diagnosis?

No. The tips are first-aid and decision guidance. They help you avoid obvious mistakes and send better information, but a final repair quote still depends on the exact device, symptoms, parts, and condition.

What if I cannot find my exact device problem?

Search by the symptom rather than the device model. If there is no close guide, text the model, what happened, current symptoms, and photos to Hailey Device Repair for a direct answer.
