Packing Guide

How to pack an iPad for shipping

iPads ship easily if you pad them well. The big mistake is sending one loose in an oversized box with one crumpled paper towel and a prayer.

power_settings_new

Power it off

Power the iPad off fully before you ship it. Remove any keyboard case, pencil, or accessories.

package_2

Use a snug box

Smaller iPads often fit fine in a USPS Small Flat Rate box. Larger iPad Pro models may need Medium Flat Rate or another sturdy box.

layers

Wrap the glass well

Use at least 2 layers of bubble wrap and fill all empty space so the iPad cannot move around in transit.

note_alt

Put a note inside

Include your name, return address, phone number, issue, and passcode on a note inside the box — not on the outside.

iPad Mail-In FAQ

Questions people ask before shipping an iPad

How much does mail-in iPad repair cost in Idaho?expand_more

Most mail-in iPad jobs land between $89 and $499+, depending on the model and whether the issue is glass only, full display, battery, or charging. Standard iPads usually land lower than Air and Pro models.

Can you fix a cracked iPad that still works?expand_more

Yes. If the LCD underneath is still fine and the glass is what took the hit, that is often one of the better iPad mail-in repairs. Send a photo and I can usually tell quickly which side of that line it is on.

How long does mail-in iPad repair take?expand_more

Most Idaho iPad mail-ins take about 3–5 total days door to door. Straightforward jobs are usually completed within a day of arrival. Larger or less common models can take a bit longer if parts are less common.

Is it worth repairing an older iPad?expand_more

Often yes — especially if it still gets updates and the rest of the device is fine. But I'll be honest if the repair starts getting too close to the value of the device.

Do I need to erase my iPad before mailing it in?expand_more

No. You do not need to erase it for normal glass, battery, display, or charging repairs. I do not wipe data without asking first.

Do I need to include my passcode?expand_more

Yes — put it on a note inside the box so I can test the repaired iPad before it ships back. It is only used to verify the repair worked.

What is the cheapest way to ship an iPad for repair?expand_more

For smaller iPads, USPS Small Flat Rate often works well. For larger iPad Pro models, USPS Medium Flat Rate is usually the safer bet. The goal is snug, padded, and insured enough to sleep well.

What should I text first to get an iPad quote?expand_more

Text your exact model, what broke, whether the display still shows a picture, and a photo if you can. Example: "iPad 9th gen, front glass shattered, LCD still looks normal, Twin Falls."
