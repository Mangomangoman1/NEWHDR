Packing Guide

How to pack an iPhone for shipping

If you pack it well, shipping is easy. This is the safest low-hassle way to send an iPhone from anywhere in Idaho.

power_settings_new

Power it off

Power off the iPhone before you ship it. Remove the case and any bulky accessories.

package_2

Use a small Priority box

USPS Small Flat Rate is usually the cleanest option for iPhones — about $8.15 and easy to find at any post office.

layers

Bubble wrap it well

Use at least 2 layers of bubble wrap and fill any empty space so the phone cannot rattle inside the box.

lock

Include a note inside

Add your name, return address, phone number, issue, and passcode on a note inside the box — never on the outside.

iPhone Mail-In FAQ

Questions people ask before shipping an iPhone

How much does mail-in iPhone repair cost in Idaho?expand_more

Most mail-in iPhone jobs land between $69 and $249+, depending on the model and the part needed. Batteries usually start lower. OLED screens and back glass run higher. You get a firm quote before you ship.

How long does mail-in iPhone repair take?expand_more

Most Idaho iPhone mail-ins take 3–4 total days door to door. Boise and Meridian are often faster. More remote areas can take a day longer. Most repairs are completed within 24 hours of arrival.

Can you fix cracked back glass on iPhone by mail?expand_more

Yes. Back glass is one of the most common mail-in iPhone jobs, especially on iPhone 11 and newer models. Text your exact model and a photo and I'll tell you if it makes sense to ship.

Should I erase my iPhone before mailing it in?expand_more

No. You do not need to erase your iPhone for a standard screen, battery, charging-port, or back-glass repair. I do not wipe data without asking first.

Do I need to send my passcode?expand_more

Yes — include it on a note inside the box. I need it so I can fully test the phone after the repair. It's only used to verify the repair worked.

What is the cheapest way to ship an iPhone for repair?expand_more

USPS Small Flat Rate Priority Mail is usually the cheapest and simplest option. It costs about $8.15 and gets to Hailey fast from most Idaho cities.

Is mail-in worth it from Boise or Meridian?expand_more

Often yes. A lot of Treasure Valley customers use mail-in when they want a real quote first, text updates, and a lower-friction option than manufacturer mail-in or big-box scheduling. Boise shipments often arrive next day.

What should I text first to get an iPhone quote?expand_more

Text your exact model, what broke, whether it still turns on, and a photo if you can. Example: "iPhone 13, front glass shattered but touch still works. Boise. Need it back this week."
