Packing Guide

How to pack a MacBook for shipping

MacBooks need better packing than phones. If you cushion it right, shipping is simple. If you throw it in a laptop sleeve and hope, it is not.

power_settings_new

Power it off and close the lid

Power the MacBook off fully before you ship it. Remove any hard shell case that might crack in transit.

package_2

Use a box with real padding

Choose a box at least 2 inches larger on all sides than the MacBook itself and pad the corners well.

laptop_chromebook

Include the charger if charging is part of the issue

If the problem is charging, battery, or intermittent power, include the charger in a separate bag inside the box.

note_alt

Put a note inside

Include your name, return address, phone number, issue, and login password or test-user instructions on a note inside the box — never outside.

MacBook Mail-In FAQ

Questions people ask before shipping a MacBook

How much does mail-in MacBook repair cost in Idaho?expand_more

Most mail-in MacBook jobs land between $99 and $749+, depending on the model and the part needed. Batteries and charging issues usually land lower. Displays and liquid-related problems run higher. You get a firm quote before you ship.

How long does mail-in MacBook repair take?expand_more

Most Idaho MacBook mail-ins take about 3–5 total days door to door. Straightforward battery, charging, and keyboard jobs are often finished within a day of arrival. Display jobs can take a bit longer depending on the part.

Do you work on Apple Silicon MacBooks (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)?expand_more

Yes. MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, Intel and Apple Silicon. I'll tell you fast if the issue is a good fit for mail-in or if it needs a different path.

Is a MacBook battery replacement worth it?expand_more

Usually yes. If the machine still does what you need and the battery is the weak link, a battery job is almost always cheaper than replacing the laptop.

Can you fix a liquid-damaged MacBook by mail?expand_more

Sometimes — but this is the one where honesty matters most. If the spill caused a straightforward part issue, mail-in can still make sense. If it looks like deeper board-level damage, I'll tell you that early instead of dragging you through a maybe.

Do I need to send my login password?expand_more

If the MacBook still boots to login, include the password or test-user instructions on a note inside the box so I can verify the repair fully. If you are not comfortable with that, say so when you text me and we'll figure out the cleanest workaround.

What is the cheapest way to ship a MacBook for repair?expand_more

USPS Medium Flat Rate is usually the simplest baseline at about $16.10, but any sturdy box with real padding works. For a more expensive MacBook, extra insurance is smart.

What should I text first to get a MacBook quote?expand_more

Text your exact model or year, what broke, whether it still powers on, and a photo if you can. Example: "MacBook Air M1, battery health bad, dies in 90 minutes, Boise."
