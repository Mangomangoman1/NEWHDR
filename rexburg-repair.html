Fastest Quote

What to text me so I can quote it fast

A good first message saves time. It tells me whether the repair makes sense, what range you’re probably in, and whether mail-in is the right move.

badge

Exact model

“iPhone 13” or “MacBook Air M1” is good. “Phone” or “laptop” is not.

heart_broken

What actually broke

Cracked screen, battery issue, no charge, bad keyboard, back glass, water exposure — be specific.

power_settings_new

Current symptoms

Does it still power on? Does touch still work? Any lines, heat, swelling, or charging weirdness?

photo_camera

Photo if possible

A quick photo helps a lot — especially for cracked screens, lifted batteries, broken ports, and bent frames.

sms

Best first texts: “iPhone 13, front glass shattered, touch still works, Rexburg.” · “MacBook Air M1, battery dies in 90 minutes, BYU-Idaho student.” · “iPad 9th gen, cracked glass, LCD still normal, Rigby.”

Rexburg FAQ

Is mail-in device repair worth it from Rexburg?expand_more

Often yes. Rexburg-area mail-in makes sense when you want a real quote before committing, you use the device every day, and you care more about clarity and updates than physically walking into a shop first.

How long does shipping from Rexburg to Hailey take?expand_more

Many straightforward Rexburg-area mail-in repairs land in the 3–4 day total range door to door. Shipping timing depends on the carrier and the day you send it, but this is generally a good-fit region for mail-in.

Is this a good option for BYU-Idaho students?expand_more

Yes. This page is especially built for students and student households who depend on their phones, MacBooks, iPads, or laptops every day and want to know the likely range before spending money shipping anything.

Can I mail in an iPhone from Rexburg for screen repair?expand_more

Yes. Cracked iPhone screens, back glass, battery issues, and charging-port problems are some of the most common Rexburg-area mail-in repairs. See the iPhone mail-in page.

Can I mail in a MacBook from student housing?expand_more

Yes. MacBook battery, screen, charging, keyboard, and trackpad problems are all good fit quote-first mail-in jobs if the laptop still matters to your school or work life. See the MacBook mail-in page.

What if I’m in Sugar City or Rigby instead of Rexburg?expand_more

This page is for the wider Rexburg / Upper Valley area, not just Rexburg proper. Sugar City, Rigby, St. Anthony, and Ashton all fit the same quote-first mail-in flow.

Do I need to drive anywhere first?expand_more

No. The point of the page is to let you text first, get a likely range first, and decide whether shipping makes sense before you spend extra time or money.
